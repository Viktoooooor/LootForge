LOOTFORGE

Open your League of Legends chests in bulk with cinematic, full-screen reveals. Clean up your loot in one click and browse everything you own.

Free and open source. Runs on your own PC next to the League client. Windows.

Download Source code

What it does

  • Opens chests one at a time or all at once, with reveals that show the rarity first.
  • Disenchants, unlocks and rerolls shards, with a confirmation before anything irreversible.
  • Cleans up the inventory by rules and protects the shards you star.
  • Shows skin details with the full splash art, chromas and a SkinSpotlights video preview.
  • Browses your whole collection and keeps statistics of what dropped.

How it works

LootForge talks to the League client running on your PC and sends the same requests the client's own loot screen sends. There is no injection, no memory reading and no change to any game file. It listens on 127.0.0.1 only, has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry, and your client's token never leaves your computer. Everything it does is real, so every irreversible action asks first.

The League client API is not officially supported by Riot Games and can change at any time, so use LootForge at your own risk.
